<p>
<span data-i18n="en">I am a PhD student at the French National Centre for Scientific Research (CNRS) and Université de Lorraine, supervised by <a href="https://members.loria.fr/CGardent/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Claire Gardent</a> (CNRS Research Director; Member, French Academy of Sciences; ACL Fellow; CNRS Silver Medalist) and <a href="https://albertgatt.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Albert Gatt</a> (Professor, Utrecht University).</span>
<span data-i18n="zh">我目前是法国国家科学研究中心（CNRS）与洛林大学的博士生，导师为 <a href="https://members.loria.fr/CGardent/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Claire Gardent</a>（CNRS 研究主任、法国科学院院士、ACL Fellow、CNRS 银奖得主）和 <a href="https://albertgatt.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Albert Gatt</a>（乌得勒支大学教授）。</span>
</p>
<p>
<span data-i18n="en">My research focuses on natural language generation and multilingual NLP. Specifically, I study how large language models can better verbalize structured data (i.e., Data/KG-to-Text Generation), and how multilingual data can be leveraged more effectively for text generation — with a particular interest in the value of low-resource languages for multilingual model training and knowledge augmentation.</span>
<span data-i18n="zh">我的研究方向是自然语言生成与多语言自然语言处理。具体而言，我关注如何利用大语言模型更好地阐述（verbalize）结构化数据（即 Data/KG-to-Text Generation），以及如何更有效地利用多语言数据来提升文本生成效果——尤其关注小语种数据对多语言模型训练与知识补充的价值。</span>
</p>
